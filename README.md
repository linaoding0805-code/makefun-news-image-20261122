One image that sums up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026, built
for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
[news-image-20261122](https://make.fun/competitions/news-image-20261122).

The front page (`entry.png`, 1024 x 1024) leads with the Russian drone strike
that brought down a five-storey apartment block in Dymer, north of Kyiv, and
carries the COP31 climate talks ending in Antalya with no deal, the suspected
Ukrainian sea-drone attack that sank the Russian corvette Merkury off
Benghazi, Libya, and the glitch ESA reported in BepiColombo's braking burn a
day after the probe reached Mercury, with the day's other stories below the
fold.

## How it is made

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. The page itself was composed in the build session from
four AI illustrations drawn for the day's reported events; the pictures are
labelled as illustrations on the page.
